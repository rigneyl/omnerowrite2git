Getting started

New to Omnero?

You only need a browser and somewhere to write. These are the few things worth knowing before you begin.

How do I start?

Select Start Writing, create or open a project, and begin. No account or setup process is required.

How do I install it?

Use your browser's install or Add to Home Screen option.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once installed, Omnero Write can continue working without an internet connection.

Do I need to back up my writing?

Yes. Your writing is stored locally, so important work should be exported or backed up regularly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Omnero Write does not require an account.
Where is my writing stored?
Your writing is stored locally on the device you're using, in your browser.
Does Omnero upload my writing?
No. Your writing is stored locally and isn't uploaded to Omnero servers while you write.
What happens if I clear my browser data?
Clearing browser or site data can remove writing stored locally by Omnero. Back up important work regularly using Omnero's backup or export tools.
Can I export my writing?
Yes. You can export your writing as text or Markdown, or create a project backup.
Is Omnero Write free?
Yes. Omnero Write is free to use. No account or payment is required. If you find it useful, you can optionally support its hosting and continued development.
